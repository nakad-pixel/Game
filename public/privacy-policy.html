Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 2024

1. Information We Collect

Idle Anime Ascension collects minimal data to provide game functionality:

  • Game Progress: Saved locally in your browser (LocalStorage)
  • Analytics: Anonymous gameplay statistics (level reached, playtime)
  • Optional Cloud Saves: If enabled, game progress synced to our servers

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Save and restore your game progress
  • Improve game balance and features
  • Display leaderboards (anonymous unless you provide a name)

3. Data Storage

Your game data is stored locally in your browser. Cloud saves (optional) are encrypted and stored on secure servers.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Players under 13 should not provide any personal information.

5. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services for ads and analytics. These services have their own privacy policies.

6. Your Rights

  • Delete your data: Clear browser storage or contact us
  • Opt-out of analytics: Disable in settings menu
  • Access your data: Export save file from browser storage

7. Contact Us

For privacy concerns, contact: privacy@idleanime.game

This privacy policy may be updated periodically. Continued use constitutes acceptance of changes.
