# 🎮 Idle Anime Ascension - Complete Feature List

## ✅ Core Gameplay Features

### Combat System
- ⚔️ **Auto-Combat**: Automatic attack every 1 second (adjustable by attack speed stat)
- 🎯 **Critical Hits**: 10% base crit chance, 2x base multiplier
- 💥 **Damage Scaling**: 1.1x per level with trait multipliers
- 🌊 **Wave System**: Endless waves with exponential difficulty (1.05x health per wave)
- 🎨 **Visual Feedback**: Attack animations, hit flashes, floating damage numbers
- 🖥️ **60 FPS Rendering**: Smooth PIXI.js canvas animations

### Progression Systems
- 📊 **Leveling**: 99 levels with exponential XP requirements
- 🔼 **Ascension**: Reset to level 1 for permanent 1.5x multipliers
- 🌟 **5 Prestige Layers**: Ascension → Legendary → Mythic → Divine → Transcendent
- ⚡ **Traits**: 30+ unique traits with 4 rarity levels
- 👥 **4 Characters**: Striker (DPS), Guardian (Tank), Mystic (Support), Legendary (Hybrid)
- 🏆 **15 Achievements**: Unlock rewards for reaching milestones
- 📈 **Endless Scaling**: Visible content for 100+ hours of gameplay

### Resources & Economy
- 💰 **Gold**: Primary currency for upgrades and purchases
- 💎 **Gems**: Premium currency earned via ads and achievements
- 🎁 **Offline Earnings**: Accumulate up to 8 hours of rewards while away
- 💵 **Balanced Economy**: Fast early game, diminishing returns for retention

## 💰 Monetization Features

### Shop System
- 💎 **Gem Packs**: $0.99 - $19.99 with increasing value
- ⚡ **Boosts**: 2x Gold, 2x XP, 2x All (1-2 hours duration)
- 🎨 **Cosmetics**: 6+ skins and themes (150-500 gems each)
- 🔄 **Trait Rerolls**: 50 gems to randomize available traits
- 📺 **Ad Rewards**: Watch ads for 5 free gems

### Battle Pass
- 🎫 **30 Tiers**: Dual track (free + premium $2.99/month)
- ⏱️ **Progression**: 1 tier per ~20 minutes of play
- 🎁 **Rewards**: Gems, gold, boosts, exclusive skins
- 💰 **High Value**: $15+ equivalent in premium track

### Ad Integration
- 📺 **Rewarded Videos**: Earn 5 gems per view
- ⏰ **Cooldown**: 30 second minimum between ads
- 🎯 **Strategic Placement**: Non-intrusive, player-initiated
- 📊 **Tracking**: Full analytics for ad performance
